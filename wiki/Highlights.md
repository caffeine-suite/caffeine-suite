  * [Binary Line Starts](./Binary-Line-Starts.md)
  * [Optional Commas](./Optional-Commas.md)

* Streamlined Object [Structuring](./Structuring.md) and [Destructuring](./Destructuring.md)
* Seamless [Modules and CommonJs](./Modules-and-CommonJs.md) support
* Powerful [Comprehensions and Iteration](./Comprehensions-and-Iteration.md)
* [Expressive Literals](./Expressive-Literals.md) make everything easier
