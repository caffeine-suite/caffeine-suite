### Key Benefits

* Designed to take maximum advantage of the best parts of JavaScript:
  * [[Dynamic Typing]]
  * [[Functional Programming]]
  * [[Object Oriented Programming]]
  * [[Declarative Programming]]
* Beautiful, visual, expressive, minimal syntax means increased Readability and Refactorability
  * **90% less** [[Modules and CommonJs]]-specific code
  * **60% less** syntax than JavaScript
  * **10% less** syntax than CoffeeScript
* Eliminates most of [JavaScript's rough edges](Coming-from-JavaScript), so you no longer have to feel like you're in a [SpaceQuest Adventure](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z2fmsXzXYA4).
* CaffeineScript, the language itself, is extensible on a per-file or per-project basis with the help of [CaffeineMC](https://github.com/shanebdavis/caffeine-mc).
